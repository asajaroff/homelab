* Network Overview

Ituzaingo environment network design based on Ubiquiti hardware with VLAN segmentation.

** Hardware

| Device                        | Role              |
|-------------------------------+-------------------|
| Ubiquiti Cloud Gateway Max    | Firewall / Router |
| Ubiquiti Lite 16 PoE Switch   | Core Switch       |
| Ubiquiti U7 Pro Access Point  | WiFi              |

** VLANs

| VLAN | Name       | Subnet            | Purpose                    |
|------+------------+-------------------+----------------------------|
|    1 | Management | 192.168.80.0/24   | Network device management  |
|   10 | Videocams  | 192.168.81.0/24   | Security cameras (isolated)|
|   20 | WiFi       | 192.168.82.0/24   | Wireless clients           |
|   30 | Servers    | 192.168.83.0/24   | NAS and server             |

** Firewall Considerations

- VLAN 10 (Videocams): Block internet access, allow traffic only to NAS on VLAN 30 (NVR port)
- VLAN 20 (WiFi): Standard internet access, isolated from VLAN 10 and VLAN 30
- VLAN 30 (Servers): Restricted internet access, accept inbound from VLAN 10 on NVR ports
- Management VLAN: Accessible only from trusted devices
